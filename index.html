About Lance Wade Fresquez

Lance Fresquez is a small-town gentleman who loves his family and friends more than anything. His journey in writing was inspired by his beloved son, Owen. After a life-altering diagnosis of multiple sclerosis in December 2022, he wrote his debut book Carry Me, Dad to leave his son a meaningful message about the adversities of life.

His second book, Through Maya's Eyes, is dedicated to children with physical and learning differences — a story about a blind chocolate lab named Maya who sees the strength and beauty in all her friends.

There's also Walk with Me, another heartfelt story that continues the journey of walking through life's moments together.

These stories are tender, real, and yes... they might make you cry. That's kind of the point of this game.
